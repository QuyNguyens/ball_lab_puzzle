Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ball Lab Puzzle ("the App") handles information when you use the game. The App is published and maintained by an individual developer ("we", "us", or "our").

1. Information Stored on Your Device

Ball Lab Puzzle stores certain game-related information locally on your device to provide its gameplay features. This information may include:

  • Game progress and unlocked levels.
  • Level completion results and stars earned.
  • In-game lives, hints, and undo counters.
  • Your active game session.
  • Sound and other gameplay preferences.

This information is stored locally using the Hive storage system. The App does not require you to create an account, and we do not operate a game server to synchronize your progress.

If you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App, locally stored game data may be permanently deleted. Device backup and restore behavior may depend on your operating system and device settings.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Some advertisements may be optional rewarded ads that allow you to receive in-game rewards after watching an advertisement.

Google and its advertising partners may collect or process certain information to provide, measure, and improve advertising services. Depending on your device, settings, location, and applicable requirements, this information may include:

  • IP address.
  • Device and advertising identifiers.
  • Advertising interactions and information about ad views.
  • App usage, diagnostics, and technical information.

Such information is handled by Google and its partners under their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

For more information, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Mobile Ads Data Disclosure

3. How We Use Information

Locally stored game information is used to:

  • Save and restore your game progress.
  • Maintain your current gameplay session.
  • Remember your gameplay preferences.
  • Provide the core features of the App.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not operate a system that collects or stores your game progress on our own servers. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party advertising providers, including Google Mobile Ads, may process information as described in Section 2.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Game data stored locally remains on your device until it is deleted by you, removed when the App is uninstalled, or otherwise removed by your device's storage management.

We do not maintain a separate account-based game profile or cloud save system for Ball Lab Puzzle.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the App functioning as intended. However, no method of electronic storage or data transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Ball Lab Puzzle does not require users to submit personal information directly to the developer.

Advertising services integrated into the App may process information subject to their own policies and applicable age-related requirements. We do not knowingly use the App to collect personal information directly from children.

8. Your Choices

You can stop using the App at any time. You can also clear the App's local storage or uninstall it to remove locally stored game data.

You may be able to manage advertising personalization and privacy choices through your Android device settings and Google's available privacy controls.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, third-party services, or applicable requirements. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
